A university discussion about a book on multilingualism opened a practical question for AI education. How well can a system respond when people express the same request in different languages and cultural contexts?

## Try it in class

Ask an AI assistant to explain the same concept in English and in another language you know well. Use the same audience, level of detail, and example in both prompts. Then compare three things:

1. Did both answers preserve the core idea?
2. Did the example make sense in both contexts?
3. Which explanation would a beginner actually use?

Ask a fluent speaker to review each answer. Keep the original prompts alongside their feedback so you can see whether a problem came from the question or the response.
