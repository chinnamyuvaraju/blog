Dr. Yuvaraju Chinnam works in higher education and computer science. This blog brings together short lessons for students, notes on AI research, and reflections on teaching.

## Start with the AI lessons

The short lessons pair an introductory idea with something to try. The Python note starts with a prediction project. The FastAPI note takes the next step and shows how to make a Python function available to another application.

## Research and teaching

The research notes explain ideas from papers and point to the original publications. The social archive connects selected LinkedIn posts and Instagram reels with longer notes and resources.
